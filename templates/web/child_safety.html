Pet Nabor Child Safety Standards

Pet Nabor app, operated by PET NABOR, INC · Last updated October 20, 2026

Pet Nabor connects neighbors who love animals — for pet sitting, play dates and community. Keeping children safe is a responsibility we take seriously. These standards apply to everyone who uses the Pet Nabor app and its services, and to all content shared on Pet Nabor: profiles, posts, stories, comments, chat messages, photos and videos.

Pet Nabor has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Any such content or behavior is removed, the accounts involved are permanently banned, and it is reported to the authorities.

1. What is prohibited

On Pet Nabor you may not, under any circumstances:

  • Create, upload, share, request, link to or store child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, including drawn, animated, edited or AI-generated imagery.
  • Sexualize minors in any way — sexual or suggestive comments, images, messages or poses involving anyone under 18.
  • Groom a child: build a relationship with, or contact, a minor (or someone you believe to be one) with the intent of sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Solicit, coerce or blackmail a minor for sexual content or contact (including "sextortion").
  • Engage in, promote or facilitate child trafficking or the commercial sexual exploitation of children.
  • Use Pet Nabor's features — chat, meetups, pet sitting or tips — to gain access to a child for any harmful purpose.
  • Share content that normalizes, encourages or instructs on the abuse or exploitation of children.

2. How we respond

When we become aware of content or behavior that violates these standards, we:

  • Remove the content immediately.
  • Permanently ban the accounts involved, and prevent them from returning.
  • Preserve relevant information as required by law.
  • Report apparent child sexual abuse material and exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) through its CyberTipline, and to other law enforcement authorities as required by applicable law.
  • Cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations.

Reports about child safety are treated as our highest priority.

3. How to report

In the app — every report is reviewed by our team (within 24 hours, child safety reports first):

  • A post, story or profile: tap the ⋯ menu → Report.
  • A comment or chat message: press and hold it → Report.
  • A person: open their profile → ⋯ → Report user. You can also Block them so they can no longer contact you.

By e-mail: write to our child safety contact below. You don't need a Pet Nabor account to report.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency services first (911 in the United States). You can also report directly to the NCMEC CyberTipline (United States) or the Internet Watch Foundation (international).

4. Keeping our community safe

  • Users can report and block any user, post, story, comment or message from within the app.
  • Our moderators review reports, remove violating content and suspend or ban accounts.
  • Meetups and pet sitting arranged through Pet Nabor are between adults. Never use them to contact or meet a child.
  • These standards are part of our Terms of Use, which every user agrees to.

5. Compliance with the law

Pet Nabor complies with applicable child safety laws and regulations, including the obligation to report child sexual abuse material to the authorities.
