What is deleted

  • Your account, profile and sign-in (including Google or Apple)

What is kept

  • Tips you sent or received, as payment records without your name or account
  • Chat messages you already sent, without your name or profile

Deletion is immediate and can't be undone. If you still have a tip balance or a payout in progress, please withdraw it in the app first.
