Delete your PetNabor account

You can permanently delete your PetNabor account and its data, from the app or from this page.

In the app

  1. Open PetNabor and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

On this page

Enter the email address of your account. We'll email you a link to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account, profile and sign-in (including Google or Apple sign-in)
  • Your pets, posts, stories, comments, likes and photos
  • Your friends list, meetings, notifications and saved location

What is kept

  • Tips you sent or received are kept as payment records, without your name or account.
  • Chat messages you already sent stay visible to the people you sent them to, without your name or profile.

Deletion is immediate and can't be undone. If you still have a tip balance or a payout in progress, please withdraw it in the app first.

Questions? Email support@petneighborbrands.com.
